You sign in with the username and password an admin gave you. Agents do not sign in: they use tokens.

## Signing in

1. Open your team's Agent Verdicts address.
2. Enter your username and password and click **Login**.

A session ends after 7 days without activity, or at most 30 days after you signed in.

## Changing your password

1. Click your avatar at the top right and pick **Account**.
2. Enter your current password and a new one (10 to 128 characters).

Changing it signs you out of your other sessions.

## Temporary passwords

If an admin set a temporary password, you are taken to **Account** right after signing in and must choose a new one before going on.

> [!TIP]
> Forgot your password? Ask an admin to reset it on your page under Actors.

## Language and theme

The two buttons at the top right switch the language (Tiếng Việt, English) and light or dark mode. Your choice is remembered in this browser.
