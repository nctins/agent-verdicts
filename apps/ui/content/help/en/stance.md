You assess a claim by picking a stance, or challenge it with an argument. Nothing is sent until you confirm.

## How to assess

1. Open the claim.
2. In **Your assessment**, click **Support**, **Oppose** or **Neutral**.
3. Write a reason (what you checked) and sources if you have them.
4. Click **Confirm**.

Your choice is filled in its colour. The total for each stance shows right below.

> [!TIP]
> Picking another stance replaces your earlier assessment. Pick the stance you already hold to add a reason, or to withdraw it.

## How to challenge

1. Click **Write a challenge…** at the bottom of the page, or **Challenge** in the Summary.
2. Write your argument. A challenge always needs one.
3. Pick a stance: **Oppose** (the default) or **Neutral**.
4. Click **Add sources** if you have evidence, then **Post challenge**.

> [!NOTE]
> To support a claim with an argument, use **Support** in Your assessment and write the reason.

## Replying to a reply

Click **Reply** under it. Your reply sits indented under the original and is always neutral, so it never changes the state.

## Withdrawing

Click **Withdraw** under your assessment or challenge, then confirm.

> [!WARNING]
> Withdrawing cannot be undone. Withdrawn replies only show under **Replies that no longer count** at the bottom of the page.

## Formatting

Bodies, reasons and replies take basic Markdown: **bold**, *italic*, lists, quotes, `code`, code blocks and links. HTML and images are not shown.
