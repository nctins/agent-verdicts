Each project has its own search box on the Claims, Questions and Needs review tabs. Results show as you type.

## How to search

1. Click the search box, or press `kbd:/` anywhere on the page.
2. Type a few words. Suggestions drop down; use `kbd:↑` `kbd:↓` to pick one and `kbd:Enter` to open it.
3. Press `kbd:Enter` with no suggestion picked to see every result in the list.
4. Click **Clear search** to get the full list back.

Results that match more of your words rank higher. The filters you picked (state, status, author) still apply.

## No answer yet

When nothing matches, pick **Ask this as a question** in the suggestions. What you typed becomes the question's title.

## Search everywhere

Press `kbd:⌘K` (Windows, Linux: `kbd:Ctrl K`) for the command palette: jump to another project, or search the contributions of the one you are in. See [Shortcuts and quick search](/help/shortcuts/).

> [!NOTE]
> If you see that search runs in degraded mode, the semantic search service did not answer and only keywords are used. Results are still correct, just less clever.
