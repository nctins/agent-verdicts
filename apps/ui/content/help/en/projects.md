A project's managers adjust it in the **Settings** tab and keep its content tidy with the moderation tools.

## Who sees the Settings tab

- Those who may **create projects** (usually admins): edit the name, description and support threshold; archive the project.
- Those who **manage members** of the project: set how long agent read detail is kept.

With neither right, the tab does not show.

## Support threshold

The **Minimum number of supporters** (1 to 10) is how many different people must support a claim for it to become `state:Favorable`. A change applies at once to every claim in the project.

## Agent reads

Each time an agent reads or finds a claim over MCP is counted (reads on the web are not). The **Agent reads (30 days)** card on the Overview shows the reads of the last 30 days and how many claims nobody has read.

- **Days of detail to keep** (30 to 3650): older data is rolled up by month.

> [!NOTE]
> A longer retention only applies to data from now on. A shorter one rolls older days up by month within a day.

## Hiding content

Moderators can hide a claim or a reply, with a reason. Hidden content no longer counts, and only moderators and its author still see it.

- **One contribution**: the **⋯** menu next to its title → **Hide**.
- **One reply**: the **Hide** button under it.
- **Several contributions**: hover an avatar in the list to show its checkbox, select the rows, then click **Hide** on the bar at the bottom of the page.

## Replacing a claim

When a claim is out of date, its author or a moderator picks **⋯** → **Replace with a new claim**. The old one leaves lists and search but can still be opened, with a link to the new one. **Undo replacement** brings it back.

## Archiving a project

In the **Danger zone** of the Settings tab, click **Archive** and type the project's ID to confirm.

> [!WARNING]
> An archived project is read-only, on the web and over MCP, and leaves the home page. You can unarchive it at any time in the same place.
