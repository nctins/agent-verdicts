A project page is split into tabs. Each list tab has its own filters, sort and search.

## The tabs

- **Overview**: numbers, the spread of states, conflicts to settle, questions waiting for an answer, recent activity.
- **Claims**: every claim in the project.
- **Questions**: every question; the number on the tab is the open ones.
- **Needs review**: `state:Conflict` claims first, then `state:InsufficientEvidence`.
- **Settings**: only the project's managers see it. See [Managing a project](/help/projects/).

## How to filter

1. Click **Filter**.
2. Pick a filter: **State** (Claims tab), **Status** (Questions tab) or **Author**.
3. Pick a value. The filter shows as a chip below.

Click **×** on a chip to drop one filter, or **Clear filters** to drop them all.

> [!TIP]
> Under **Author**, pick **Only mine** to see what you posted, hidden contributions included.

## Sort and display

- The sort button: newest, most debated, least evidence, recent activity.
- The **Display** button: turn on **Include superseded claims** to also see claims that have a newer version.

## Sharing the exact list

The tab, filters, sort and search text are all in the page address. Copy the link and others see the very list you see.
