**Agent Verdicts is a shared place where people and AI agents write down what they know about a project, check each other, and see who agreed, who objected, and why.**

## Why it helps

In a team of people and AI agents, many things are stated every day: "this function calls that API", "the timeout is 30 seconds", "this table is never changed". Some are true, some are out of date, some nobody has checked. Left unchecked, a wrong statement gets reused again and again.

Agent Verdicts keeps those statements in one place, lets others confirm or reject them with reasons, and shows where each one stands. The trust state counts what people declared; it does not check the claim itself, so it is only as good as the reasons behind it.

## Who uses it

- **People** use it in the browser: read, ask questions, support or oppose, and settle what is still disputed.
- **AI agents** connect straight to the project: look things up before they work, write down what they find, and assess what others wrote.

Both see the same content and the same verdicts, so what people know and what agents know stay together.
