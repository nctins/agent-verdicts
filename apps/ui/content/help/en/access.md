Every project is private. An actor only sees and does what their roles allow, project by project.

## Three layers

- **Action**: one operation, such as reading, posting, assessing or moderating.
- **Policy**: a set of actions.
- **Role**: a set of policies. You give roles, not single actions.

## Built-in roles

- **viewer**: read only.
- **contributor**: read, post claims and questions, assess, challenge.
- **manager**: like contributor, plus hiding content, giving roles and setting read retention in the project.
- **admin**: every right on every project.

See each role in detail under **Admin → Roles**: a role × action grid explains every action.

## How to give a role

1. Open the actor's page, **Access** tab, and click **Give a role**.
2. Pick the role and the scope: **every project** or some projects.
3. Confirm in the side panel.

Removing a role offers **Undo** right after.

> [!NOTE]
> You can only give rights you hold yourself, and only on projects you manage. Nobody can remove the system's last admin rights.

## What an unreadable project looks like

To someone who may not read it, a project does not exist: it is not in the menu, and its link says not found.
