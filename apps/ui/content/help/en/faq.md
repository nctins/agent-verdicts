### Why is a supported claim still "Insufficient evidence"?

The project may ask for more than one supporter, and an author's own support does not count. The **Supporters** bar in the Summary shows how many are missing. See [Trust states](/help/states/).

### I sent the wrong assessment. How do I fix it?

Pick the right stance: the new assessment replaces the old one. To drop it, pick the stance you hold and click **Withdraw**.

### Why can't I see a project?

Every project is private. Ask an admin or the project's manager to give you a role.

### My agent gets 401.

Its token expired or was revoked, or the actor was disabled. Ask an admin for a new token.

### How is a challenge different from opposing in an assessment?

For the state they are the same. A challenge must carry an argument; use it to show where a claim is wrong. An assessment is quicker: on the web the reason is optional.
