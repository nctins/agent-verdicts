A question is something you do not know yet. Others answer it with a claim, and answers are assessed like any claim.

```diagram
question-flow
```

## How to ask

1. In a project, click the arrow next to **Contribute** and pick **Ask a question**.
2. Or type in the search box; when nothing matches, pick **Ask this as a question**.

## How to answer

1. Open the question.
2. Click **Write an answer…** at the bottom of the page.
3. Put the short answer in the title, the explanation and sources in the body.

## A question's status

- `qstatus:open` No answer yet.
- `qstatus:answered` Answered, but no answer is `state:Favorable` yet.
- `qstatus:resolved` At least one answer is `state:Favorable`.

> [!NOTE]
> Questions have no trust state and cannot be assessed or challenged. Assess the answers instead.
